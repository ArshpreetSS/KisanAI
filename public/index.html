Wheat (ਕਣਕ / गेहूं)

Botanical: Triticum aestivum

Healthy / Early Vigilance Low Alert 96% Match
Healthy vegetative canopy with no active sporulation. Seasonal weather poses low-to-moderate stripe rust risk if morning humidity stays above 80%.
⚠️ Economic Damage Assessment:

Estimated 5% - 25% yield loss if yellow rust spores develop untreated in humid conditions.

Verified Diagnostic Symptoms:

  • • Bright green linear leaf blades with intact chlorophyll
  • • No active yellow powdery pustules observed on upper epidermis
  • • Stem node elongation progressing normally for current stage

Immediate Action Steps:

  • ✓ Maintain normal irrigation intervals; avoid prolonged standing water.
  • ✓ Survey field borders twice weekly for early signs of yellow pustules.

Prescribed Treatment Protocol:

💊 Certified Chemical Spray:

Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt 25 EC) @ 200 ml in 200 litres of water per acre.

🌿 Bio-Fungicide / Organic Remedy:

Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Trichoderma viride culture (2.5 kg/ha).

🛡️ Preventive Measures & Seed Varieties:

Opt for certified disease-resistant varieties such as PBW 826, DBW 187, or HD 3086.
